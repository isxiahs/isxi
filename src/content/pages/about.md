## Archival Manifesto

Nocturne Monograph operates as an open-access frequency imprint and architectural publishing house. We document intersections between subterranean sound synthesis, brutalist spatiality, and critical material culture.

### Method & Preservation

Every text is published simultaneously in physical print using exposed Swiss binding and digital preservation formats. Our audio releases are mastered directly from analog tape loops to preserve harmonic overtones and tactile tape drift.
